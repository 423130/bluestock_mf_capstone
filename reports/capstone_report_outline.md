# Bluestock MF Analytics Platform: Professional Capstone Report Outline

**Project Name:** Bluestock MF Analytics Platform  
**Scope:** Comprehensive Mutual Fund Performance & Investor Behavior Analysis  
**Date:** June 2026

---

## 1. Executive Summary
*   **Overview:** A high-level summary of the Bluestock MF Analytics Platform, designed to provide 360-degree visibility into the Indian Mutual Fund ecosystem.
*   **Key Highlights:** 
    *   Analysis of **40 premium schemes** across **10 fund houses**.
    *   Processing of **46,000+ daily NAV records** and **32,000+ investor transactions**.
    *   Identification of industry-wide growth: **26.12 Cr total folios**.
    *   Peak SIP Inflow record: **₹31,002 Cr (Dec 2025)**.

## 2. Project Objectives
*   **Primary Goal:** Build an automated end-to-end data pipeline to evaluate fund performance and investor behavior.
*   **Sub-objectives:**
    *   Implement a robust ETL process for heterogeneous financial datasets.
    *   Calculate advanced risk-adjusted metrics (Sharpe, Sortino, Alpha, Beta).
    *   Analyze geographic and demographic trends across **5,000 unique investors** and **12 Indian states**.
    *   Develop a composite "Fund Scorecard" for investor decision support.

## 3. Data Sources & Integration
*   **Primary Sources:** 
    *   **AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India):** Historical AUM and Folio data.
    *   **mfapi.in:** Real-time and historical NAV feeds.
    *   **Bluestock Internal Datasets:** 10 curated CSV files covering Fund Masters, Transactions, and Portfolio Holdings.
*   **Volume:** 46,000+ NAV data points; 32,778 transaction records; coverage of **Nifty 50** and **Nifty 100** benchmarks.

## 4. ETL Pipeline & Data Architecture
*   **Architecture:** Modular Python-based ETL using `Pandas` and `SQLAlchemy`.
*   **Star Schema Design:**
    *   `dim_fund`: Master attributes of 40 schemes.
    *   `dim_date`: Temporal dimension for time-series slicing.
    *   `fact_nav`: Daily price history and returns.
    *   `fact_transactions`: Granular investor activity.
    *   `fact_aum`: Periodic AMC-level assets.
    *   `fact_performance`: Pre-computed analytical metrics.
*   **Storage:** SQLite (`bluestock_mf.db`) for high-concurrency dashboard support.

## 5. EDA: Key Findings & Market Insights
*   **Insight 1: The SIP Revolution:** Monthly SIP inflows showing exponential growth, peaking at ₹31,002 Cr.
*   **Insight 2: Concentration of Wealth:** **SBI Mutual Fund** leads the market with a dominant AUM of **₹12.5L Cr**.
*   **Insight 3: The 2023 Bull Run:** Visualization of the 2023 market rally and its impact on Small vs. Large Cap NAVs.
*   **Insight 4: Folio Expansion:** Industry-wide jump from 13.26 Cr to 26.12 Cr folios within the study period.
*   **Insight 5: Category Preference:** Heatmap evidence showing massive retail shift toward Flexi-cap and Mid-cap categories.

## 6. Performance Analytics & Risk Audit
*   **Metric Summary:**
    *   **Alpha/Beta:** Regression results showing the "Active Return" generated by fund managers against the Nifty 50.
    *   **Sharpe & Sortino:** Evaluation of risk-adjusted returns (Rf = 6.5%).
    *   **Tail Risk:** **VaR (95%)** and **CVaR** analysis identifying maximum potential losses during market stress.
*   **The Bluestock Scorecard:** Ranking funds based on a weighted blend of 3yr CAGR (30%), Sharpe (25%), Alpha (20%), Expense Ratio (15%), and Max Drawdown (10%).

## 7. Dashboard Overview (4-Page Concept)
*   **Page 1: Executive C-Suite Dashboard:** Macro trends, Total AUM, SIP Growth, and Top 5 Funds.
*   **Page 2: Fund Manager Deep Dive:** Detailed performance metrics (Alpha, Beta, Rolling Sharpe) for individual schemes.
*   **Page 3: Investor Demographic Hub:** State-wise heatmaps (12 states), age distribution pie charts, and city-tier (T30/B30) analysis.
*   **Page 4: Risk & Compliance Console:** VaR reports, Sector HHI (concentration) index, and SIP continuity/lapse tracking.

## 8. Investor Demographic Insights
*   **Core Segments:** The 26-35 age group emerges as the most active "Digital First" segment.
*   **Geographic Spread:** Analyzing the penetration of mutual funds in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities (B30 segment).
*   **Continuity:** Identifying a **3.5% lapse rate** in SIPs with gaps > 35 days, suggesting potential areas for customer retention strategies.

## 9. Benchmark Comparison Results
*   **Alpha Generation:** Analysis of what percentage of the 40 schemes successfully beat the Nifty 50 TRI.
*   **Tracking Error:** Evaluation of how closely passive/large-cap funds follow the Nifty 100 benchmark.
*   **Relative Strength:** Visual comparison of Top 5 Scorers against the broader market indices.

## 10. Limitations & Recommendations
*   **Limitations:**
    *   Reliance on historical data which may not predict future volatility.
    *   Exclusion of Tax implications (LTCG/STCG) in return calculations.
*   **Recommendations:**
    *   Implement real-time API polling for NAV updates.
    *   Add "Predictive Churn" modeling for SIP investors using Machine Learning.
    *   Expand to include ESG ratings for fund analysis.

## 11. Conclusion
*   **Final Statement:** The Bluestock MF Analytics Platform successfully transforms raw financial data into actionable investment intelligence.
*   **Impact:** Empowers both AMCs to understand their investors and retail users to make data-driven decisions via the Bluestock Scorecard.
